Brief me.

RingMeFor is a notification alarm for iPhone. It rings a real alarm, even on Silent and in a Focus, when a notification from an app you chose has the words or the people you care about, and keeps everything else quiet.

Price
Free for one app with one rule. RingMeFor Pro is $4.99, paid once, with no subscription.
Needs
iPhone with iOS 27 or later.
