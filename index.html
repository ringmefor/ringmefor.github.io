Tell me how it works.

iOS doesn't let one app read another app's notifications, so RingMeFor works through Apple's Shortcuts app. The RingMeFor shortcut's automation hands each notification from your chosen apps to RingMeFor. RingMeFor checks it against your rules, on your iPhone. If it matches, an iOS alarm rings with Stop and Snooze on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island; if not, nothing happens.

  1. SSlack, #incidentsCheckout outage, paging on call A notification arrives
  2. Shortcuts hands it to RingMeFor
  3. RingMeFor checks your rules
  4. It ringsor it stays quiet

Set it up

  1. Get RingMeFor and choose your apps, then the words and people that matter in each.
  2. Add the RingMeFor shortcut, and add your apps to it with +.
  3. Turn on its automation: tap the arrow after its apps. It arrives switched off, and only you can turn it on.
  4. Lock your iPhone and run Test with the Phone Locked.

Tell me what it can't do.

What it can't do

  • Turn on its own automation. You do that once in Shortcuts, because Apple doesn't let an app do it.
  • Ring after a restart before you unlock. Until your iPhone has been unlocked once, nothing rings.
  • Hear what isn't delivered. A Focus or Scheduled Summary that holds an app's notifications back keeps them from RingMeFor too.
  • Promise every run. iOS can skip a run, so RingMeFor is a backup, not a medical or safety alarm.

How it checks itself

  • Test with the Phone Locked runs the whole path, from a notification to an alarm.
  • An optional nightly check tells you if the shortcut stops answering.
  • Why Didn't It Ring? looks at the shortcut, the app's rules and the alarm permission.
  • Test a Message shows whether a message you type would ring.
  • History shows what rang and what was skipped, and why.

Keep it on my iPhone.

No account, no server, no internet needed. RingMeFor checks your notifications on your iPhone, and your rules and History stay there. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

How does it know when a notification arrives?

Through Apple's Shortcuts app. iOS 27 lets a Shortcuts automation run when a notification arrives, and RingMeFor's ready-made shortcut passes the notification on. You add it once; RingMeFor shows each step.

Will it ring when my iPhone is on Silent or in a Focus?

Yes. RingMeFor rings with iOS's own alarms, which sound through Silent mode and Focus, like the Clock app's.

Does it read or keep my messages?

RingMeFor checks each notification against your rules on your iPhone and keeps a short History there. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else; iCloud sync (1.0.1 and later) uses your own iCloud account. Read the privacy policy.

What if it didn't ring?

Open the app and tap “Why didn't it ring?”: it checks the shortcut, the app's rules and the alarm permission. The help page covers the rest.

Doesn't Focus already do this?

Focus chooses who can reach you by app or by person. It can't choose by what a message says, and what it lets through is still an ordinary notification, which makes one sound, or none on Silent. RingMeFor chooses by the words in the message as well, and it rings an alarm.
